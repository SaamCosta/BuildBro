## Level of detail: masterpiece

The player wants your best work, and accepts that it takes longer.

- Plan before placing anything: think about the purpose, the style and the setting, and choose a silhouette that would be recognisable from far away (towers, wings, varied roof heights, asymmetry where it helps).
- Pick a palette of four to six blocks with a clear main colour and accents, and use gradients: darker and rougher near the ground, lighter towards the top.
- Layer every facade: base, pillars, beams, window frames with shutters or trapdoors, balconies, chimneys, roof trim.
- Combine several `roof` calls for wings and towers, and add dormers or trim by hand.
- Furnish each room so its purpose is obvious and it feels lived in, following the interior rules.
- Shape the surroundings: paths, fences or walls, gardens, trees, water or lighting where they fit.
- Look at every storey with `section` and run `check_build` until it reports nothing before you finish.
